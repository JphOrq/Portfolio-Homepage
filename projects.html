Integrated Tuberculosis
Information System (ITIS)
ITIS is the official web-based and mobile system of the Department of Health under the National Tuberculosis Control Program (NTP), designed to digitally record, monitor, and manage tuberculosis (TB) cases across public and private healthcare facilities.
National Tuberculosis Control Program (NTP)
NTP is the Department of Health's initiative aimed at reducing and eliminating tuberculosis (TB) through nationwide programs, policies, treatment services, and a centralized platform for TB information, resources, and surveillance.
ITIS - Private TB Notification (ITIS Lite)
ITIS Lite is a simplified online reporting platform under the Integrated Tuberculosis Information System (ITIS) of the Department of Health (DOH). It is designed specifically for private healthcare providers to report tuberculosis (TB) cases.
Holiday Spin-O-Rama

Players can spin a colorful wheel adorned with various holiday-themed prizes, from gift cards to holiday decorations. The game is designed to be easy to play, making it suitable for all ages. PasaGamit
PasaGamit is an online college marketplace designed to help students buy, sell, and trade academic and personal items within their school community. The platform provides a safe and convenient space where students from different year levels and courses can connect and exchange items such as textbooks, school uniforms, calculators, laboratory equipment, school supplies, gadgets, and other educational materials.
Won-Now
The "Wheel of Ninong-Ninang on Wheels" is a popular game in the Philippines because it is a fun and easy way for "Ninong/Ninang (godparents)" to give gifts to their "Inaanak (godchildren)". It is also a good way for "Ninong/Ninang" to bond with their "Inaanak" and create lasting memories. The game is played by spinning a wheel that is divided into different sections, each with a different prize. The player wins the prize that is in the section where the wheel lands.
